Behavioural (averaged) simulation, not switching-level. The output follows the FHA steady-state gain of the Fig. 12 model with a 0.2 ms first-order lag. A PI controller sets fs to regulate VO = 206 V, limited to 0.5–5·FR. The lag, controller gains, frequency limits and VS scaling are illustrative choices, not values from the paper. FHA has no losses, so the size of the overshoot is exaggerated, especially at large Cjc. The direction of the failure is what matters: once fs passes the gain minimum, raising it raises VO.
